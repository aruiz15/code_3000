# Personal Ethics Statement

I think that ethics within CS should really focus on both fairness and accountability.  When I think about what I will be doing in ther future, I start to realize that the systems I build will impact people's lives ways that matter even if that impact isn't immediately visible.

To me it is really important to design systems that avoid bias and respect a user's privacy. Technology shouldn't be used to benefit society and absolutely not exploit it. This includes being mindful of how data is collected and how algorithms make decisions.

Furthermore, I think that developers have a responsibility to question the ethical implications of their work rather than blindly following instructions. Let's say something is technically possible, that doen't mean it should always be built.

Reading chapter 5 of Ethical Machines made me realize that I want to aim to do my work with transparency and consider the broader societal consequences of the technology I make.
